How DNS leaks happen

Even when you configure a VPN or custom IPv4 DNS, operating systems sometimes fall back to DHCP-assigned ISP servers over IPv6 or secondary interfaces. Plaintext UDP/53 queries can also be transparently intercepted by some ISP routers.

How to fix a leak

Enable DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH) or DNS-over-TLS (DoT) so middleboxes cannot read or hijack port 53 traffic, and verify both IPv4 and IPv6 DNS assignments on your active adapter.
