Router-level vs device-level protection

Configuring a family DNS server on your home router protects laptops, tablets, game consoles, and smart TVs at once. For phones that leave home Wi-Fi, set the matching Private DNS hostname under Android or iOS settings.

SafeSearch enforcement

AdGuard Family and CleanBrowsing Family also force SafeSearch on major search engines and restricted mode on supported video platforms.
