Indian ISP routing notes

Routing quality varies widely between JioFiber, Airtel Xstream, ACT Fibernet, and BSNL. Cloudflare, Google, and Quad9 maintain local Indian Anycast nodes that avoid international detours to Singapore or Europe.

Use DoH or DoT on mobile networks

Some Indian mobile and broadband networks intercept plaintext UDP port 53 requests. Enabling Android Private DNS (DoT) or DoH in the Rust app bypasses port 53 interference.
