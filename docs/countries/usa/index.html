US peering and ISP notes

Cloudflare, Google, Quad9, and OpenDNS operate dozens of Anycast points of presence across US metros. Cable and fiber subscribers often see 5 to 20 ms median query times compared to congested ISP defaults.

Privacy under US jurisdiction

If you prefer a non-US jurisdiction with strict no-log commitments, test Quad9 (Switzerland) or Mullvad DNS (Sweden), both of which maintain fast US Anycast nodes.
