UK peering notes

Resolvers peering directly at LINX in London and Manchester typically answer UK fiber connections in under 15 ms. European privacy options such as Quad9, Mullvad, and dns0.eu also maintain low-latency UK nodes.

Encrypted DNS on UK ISPs

Using DoH or DoT prevents plaintext port 53 inspection and ensures consistent resolution across home broadband and 5G networks.
