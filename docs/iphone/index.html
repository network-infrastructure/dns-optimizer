Manual Wi-Fi DNS on iOS

Open Settings, tap Wi-Fi, tap the info icon next to your network, scroll to Configure DNS, switch from Automatic to Manual, remove the router IP, and add your chosen primary and secondary IPv4 addresses.

Encrypted DNS across cellular and Wi-Fi

Apple supports native DoH and DoT via configuration profiles (.mobileconfig) provided by Cloudflare, Quad9, Mullvad, AdGuard, and NextDNS.
