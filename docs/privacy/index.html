What makes a DNS resolver private

Look for four technical properties: an audited no-IP-logging policy, encrypted transport (DoH or DoT), QNAME minimization so upstream root servers see less of your query, and EDNS Client Subnet disabled so your approximate IP subnet is not forwarded.

Verify your setup

Run dns-optimizer test-privacy to verify which resolver answers your system queries and confirm DNSSEC validation is active.
