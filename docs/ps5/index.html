How to change DNS on PlayStation 5

Open Settings, choose Network, select Settings, and pick Set Up Internet Connection. Press the Options button on your active Wi-Fi or LAN connection, choose Advanced Settings, set DNS Settings to Manual, and enter your Primary and Secondary DNS addresses.

What to expect on PS5

Manual DNS can make PlayStation Store pages and CDN patch downloads connect more reliably when your ISP resolver is slow. It will not reduce active match ping after a game session starts.
